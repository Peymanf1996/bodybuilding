Advanced Program

3-Day TRX + Dumbbell Plan

A push / pull / legs & core split using suspension straps plus a single dumbbell. Designed for experienced users with one year of consistent TRX training.

🔗 TRX Suspension Trainer 🏋️ 1 Dumbbell 🧘 Bodyweight
Level
Advanced
Frequency
3 Days / Week
Duration
45–60 Min
Rest
60–90 Sec

Warm-Up · 5–10 Minutes

  • Shoulder circles — 1–2 minutes
  • Bodyweight squats — 15 reps
  • Arm swings — 30 seconds
  • Light TRX row — 2 × 10 reps
  • Hip hinges and leg swings — 1–2 minutes

Single Dumbbell Rule

One dumbbell, two sides: perform all reps on one side, then switch. Rest 30–45 seconds between sides. If your dumbbell is fixed-weight, increase difficulty using slower tempo, pauses, or a harder TRX body angle instead of heavier load.

Progression & Guidelines

Progression Rules

  1. Progressive Overload: each week change only one variable — reps, tempo, body angle, or dumbbell weight.
  2. Deload Week: every 4th week reduce to 2 sets and easier angles.
  3. Rotation: every 6–8 weeks swap one exercise for a harder variation.
  4. Tracking: record sets, reps, tempo, angle, and dumbbell weight.

Intensity Tools

  • Body Angle: the closer to parallel with TRX, the harder.
  • Tempo: slow eccentric phases increase time under tension.
  • Single-Limb: one arm or leg doubles the stability demand.
  • Pauses: 1–2 sec holds increase control and difficulty.

Important Notes

  • Prioritize perfect form over repetition count.
  • If form breaks, make the angle easier by stepping closer to the anchor.
  • With one dumbbell, complete all reps on one side before switching.
  • Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain.
  • Designed for experienced TRX users — adjust to your recovery and goals.
